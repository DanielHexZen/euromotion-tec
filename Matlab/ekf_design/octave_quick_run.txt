
##### design: noise models / Allan

=== Q/R mapping (all defaults are placeholders until measured) ===
ekf_gyro_nd  = angle random walk N of the projected yaw rate [rad/s/sqrt(Hz)]
               source: Allan deviation at tau = 1 s (slope -1/2), or datasheet noise
               density [mdps/sqrt(Hz)] * pi/180e3.  Q_tt += nd^2*dt while moving.
               default 1.0e-04 -> heading sigma after 100 s of driving: 0.057 deg
ekf_q_bg     = rate random walk K^2 [(rad/s)^2/s]; Allan slope +1/2: sigma(tau) = K sqrt(tau/3)
               default 1.0e-10 -> bias sigma growth over a 60 s leg: 0.0044 deg/s
ZUPT R       = nd^2/T + ekf_zupt_floor^2: variance of the mean of white rate noise over T
               T = 1.0 s -> sigma 1.06e-04 rad/s (0.0061 deg/s); one waypoint hold (10.5 s) of
               ten ZUPTs -> 3.35e-05 rad/s, floor-limited at 3.5e-05
             note: the floor is applied per ZUPT, so ten ZUPTs reduce it by sqrt(10); if the
               floor stands for a correlated error (temperature, quantisation), that
               reduction is optimistic.
ekf_gyro_sf  = gyro scale-factor uncertainty after imu_gz_scale. From the 10-turn spin of H7:
               residual delta (rad) after 20*pi rad -> sf = delta/(20 pi). H7 pass (0.5 deg):
               sf <= 1.4e-04. Default 0.003 is the UNCALIBRATED value.
               Q model (finding F-3): spec Q_tt += (sf*dth)^2 per step treats a constant
               scale error as white. For one 90 deg turn it gives variance sf^2*(pi/2)^2/n_steps
               (n_steps = 120 at 50 Hz, 480 at 200 Hz) instead of (sf*pi/2)^2:
                  50 Hz: spec sigma 0.0272 deg, true 0.270 deg, per-radian (corr 2 pi) 0.540 deg
                 200 Hz: spec sigma 0.0136 deg, true 0.270 deg, per-radian (corr 2 pi) 0.540 deg
ekf_kd       = random distance noise [m]: variance per metre. From repeated D5000 (n runs):
               kd = var(end error)/5 m AFTER removing the mean (the mean is the scale s).
               default 1.0e-04 -> sigma 22.4 mm over 5 m, 63.2 mm over 40 m
ekf_q_s      = drift of the odometry scale [1/s] (surface, load, tyre). Expected change ds over
               a run of T s: q_s = ds^2/T. default 1.0e-09 -> sigma 0.09 % over 900 s
ekf_sig_edge = sigma of the footprint position at the event: sqrt(spot^2 + ragged^2 +
               (v_lateral*timestamp error)^2). Spot: ROI 4x4 at h0 = 0.12 m -> 31.6 mm
               diameter; timestamp error +-poll/2 = 2.5 ms (negligible laterally).
ekf_slip_thr = 0.15 rad/s vs. encoder yaw-rate resolution 1 count: 0.0143 rad/s at 50 Hz, 0.0570 at 200 Hz
               and vs. systematic residuals: wheel mismatch 0.64 % at 0.45 m/s -> 0.013 rad/s,
               wheelbase error 2 % at 1.5 rad/s -> 0.030 rad/s. Margin x5.0.

Allan demo (simulated 2.0 h at 100 Hz): nd true 1.000e-04 fit 9.994e-05 (-0.1 %), q_bg true 1.00e-10 fit 7.72e-11 (-23 %)

##### design: gates

=== Gates ===
false rejection: scalar gate 9.00 -> 0.270 %, 2-D gate 9.21 -> 1.000 %
scalar outlier of k sigma (sigma = sqrt(S)) rejected with probability:
   k =  2: 0.159
   k =  3: 0.500
   k =  4: 0.841
   k =  5: 0.977
   k =  6: 0.999
   k =  8: 1.000
   k = 10: 1.000
note: the gate measures k in units of sqrt(S), not of ekf_sig_edge. After a long edge-free
  stretch S is dominated by P, so a false event 0.2 m off is accepted when the cross-track
  sigma exceeds ~0.2/3 = 7 cm; the first edge after such a stretch is the vulnerable one.
